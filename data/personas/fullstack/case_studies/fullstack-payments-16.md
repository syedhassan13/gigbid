# Subscription billing integration for a media seed-stage startup

**Client:** a seed-stage startup in media · **Duration:** 10 weeks · **Year:** 2023

## The problem
The client's team had a recurring pain: subscriptions were invoiced manually. They needed something reliable that their non-technical staff could trust.

## What I built
I designed and delivered self-serve plans, proration and dunning via webhooks. I worked with Stripe, Node.js, React and webhooks, and set it up so the client's team could run it without me. Long-running work runs in the background so users never wait.

## Outcome
Within the first months the system recovered 40% of failed payments. The client extended the engagement for a second phase.
