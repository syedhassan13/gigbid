# Two-sided marketplace MVP for a legal Series B scale-up

**Client:** a Series B scale-up in legal · **Duration:** 10 weeks · **Year:** 2024

## The problem
The client's team had a recurring pain: the founder validated demand with a spreadsheet. They needed something reliable that their non-technical staff could trust.

## What I built
I designed and delivered an MVP with listings, payouts and reviews. I worked with Next.js, Stripe, TypeScript and PostgreSQL, and set it up so the client's team could run it without me. Long-running work runs in the background so users never wait.

## Outcome
Within the first months the system processed 27k USD GMV in month one. The client extended the engagement for a second phase.
