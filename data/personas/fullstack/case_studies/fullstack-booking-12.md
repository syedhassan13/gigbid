# Appointment booking system for a fintech regional chain

**Client:** a regional chain in fintech · **Duration:** 8 weeks · **Year:** 2024

## The problem
The client's team had a recurring pain: bookings were taken by phone and often double-booked. They needed something reliable that their non-technical staff could trust.

## What I built
I designed and delivered online booking with reminders sent from a background job queue. I worked with Django, Python, React and PostgreSQL, and set it up so the client's team could run it without me. Long-running work runs in the background so users never wait.

## Outcome
Within the first months the system cut no-shows by 22%. The client extended the engagement for a second phase.
