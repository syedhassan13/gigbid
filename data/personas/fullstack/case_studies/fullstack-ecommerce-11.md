# Headless e-commerce storefront for a insurance mid-size agency

**Client:** a mid-size agency in insurance · **Duration:** 6 weeks · **Year:** 2026

## The problem
The client's team had a recurring pain: the old theme was slow on mobile. They needed something reliable that their non-technical staff could trust.

## What I built
I designed and delivered a headless storefront with server rendering and image optimisation. I worked with Next.js, React, Shopify and e-commerce, and set it up so the client's team could run it without me. Long-running work runs in the background so users never wait.

## Outcome
Within the first months the system improved mobile conversion by 40%. The client extended the engagement for a second phase.
