# CMS migration to headless for a media solo founder

**Client:** a solo founder in media · **Duration:** 10 weeks · **Year:** 2025

## The problem
The client's team had a recurring pain: a legacy WordPress site was slow and hard to edit. They needed something reliable that their non-technical staff could trust.

## What I built
I designed and delivered a headless CMS with preserved URLs and SEO metadata. I worked with WordPress, Next.js, SEO and content migration, and set it up so the client's team could run it without me. Long-running work runs in the background so users never wait.

## Outcome
Within the first months the system cut page load time by 35%. The client extended the engagement for a second phase.
