# Single sign-on and auth hardening for a real estate Series B scale-up

**Client:** a Series B scale-up in real estate · **Duration:** 8 weeks · **Year:** 2022

## The problem
The client's team had a recurring pain: enterprise customers required SSO. They needed something reliable that their non-technical staff could trust.

## What I built
I designed and delivered OAuth/OIDC SSO with SCIM provisioning and MFA. I worked with authentication, OAuth, Node.js and security, and set it up so the client's team could run it without me. Long-running work runs in the background so users never wait.

## Outcome
Within the first months the system unblocked 40 enterprise deals. The client extended the engagement for a second phase.
