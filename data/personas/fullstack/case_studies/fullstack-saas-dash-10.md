# SaaS analytics dashboard for a e-commerce mid-size agency

**Client:** a mid-size agency in e-commerce · **Duration:** 8 weeks · **Year:** 2026

## The problem
The client's team had a recurring pain: customers exported CSVs to understand their own usage. They needed something reliable that their non-technical staff could trust.

## What I built
I designed and delivered an in-app dashboard with filters, drill-downs and scheduled reports. I worked with React, TypeScript, Node.js and PostgreSQL, and set it up so the client's team could run it without me. Long-running work runs in the background so users never wait.

## Outcome
Within the first months the system reduced support tickets about reporting by 22%. The client extended the engagement for a second phase.
