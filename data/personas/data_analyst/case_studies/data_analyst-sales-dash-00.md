# Executive sales dashboard for a education consultancy

**Client:** a consultancy in education · **Duration:** 3 weeks · **Year:** 2022

## The problem
The client's team had a recurring pain: leadership waited a week for the monthly sales deck. They needed something reliable that their non-technical staff could trust.

## What I built
I designed and delivered a self-serve dashboard refreshed daily. I worked with Power BI, SQL, DAX and data visualization, and set it up so the client's team could run it without me. Long-running work runs in the background so users never wait.

## Outcome
Within the first months the system saved 18 hours of manual reporting per month. The client extended the engagement for a second phase.
