# KPI reporting layer in Looker for a media university lab

**Client:** a university lab in media · **Duration:** 3 weeks · **Year:** 2026

## The problem
The client's team had a recurring pain: KPIs were computed differently in every spreadsheet. They needed something reliable that their non-technical staff could trust.

## What I built
I designed and delivered a governed semantic layer and KPI tiles. I worked with Looker, SQL, KPI reporting and data modeling, and set it up so the client's team could run it without me. Long-running work runs in the background so users never wait.

## Outcome
Within the first months the system adopted by 31 teams within a month. The client extended the engagement for a second phase.
