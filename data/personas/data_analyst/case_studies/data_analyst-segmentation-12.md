# Customer segmentation analysis for a manufacturing family-owned business

**Client:** a family-owned business in manufacturing · **Duration:** 12 weeks · **Year:** 2021

## The problem
The client's team had a recurring pain: marketing sent the same email to everyone. They needed something reliable that their non-technical staff could trust.

## What I built
I designed and delivered behavioural segments with personas and recommended actions. I worked with Python, clustering, customer analytics and pandas, and set it up so the client's team could run it without me. Long-running work runs in the background so users never wait.

## Outcome
Within the first months the system raised email revenue by 35%. The client extended the engagement for a second phase.
