# ELT pipeline and warehouse model for a real estate university lab

**Client:** a university lab in real estate · **Duration:** 12 weeks · **Year:** 2022

## The problem
The client's team had a recurring pain: every team had its own version of revenue. They needed something reliable that their non-technical staff could trust.

## What I built
I designed and delivered a tested warehouse model with one definition of each metric. I worked with SQL, data engineering, ETL and BigQuery, and set it up so the client's team could run it without me. Long-running work runs in the background so users never wait.

## Outcome
Within the first months the system reduced metric disputes by 22%. The client extended the engagement for a second phase.
