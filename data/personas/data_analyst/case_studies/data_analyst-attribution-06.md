# Marketing attribution model for a media university lab

**Client:** a university lab in media · **Duration:** 8 weeks · **Year:** 2021

## The problem
The client's team had a recurring pain: budget was split by gut feeling. They needed something reliable that their non-technical staff could trust.

## What I built
I designed and delivered multi-touch attribution with a channel ROI dashboard. I worked with marketing analytics, SQL, Google Analytics and Looker, and set it up so the client's team could run it without me. Long-running work runs in the background so users never wait.

## Outcome
Within the first months the system reallocated budget for a 52% lower CAC. The client extended the engagement for a second phase.
