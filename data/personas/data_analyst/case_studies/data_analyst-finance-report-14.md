# Automated financial reporting for a education solo founder

**Client:** a solo founder in education · **Duration:** 4 weeks · **Year:** 2022

## The problem
The client's team had a recurring pain: month-end close took nine days. They needed something reliable that their non-technical staff could trust.

## What I built
I designed and delivered automated reconciliations and a reporting pack. I worked with Excel, financial reporting, SQL and automation, and set it up so the client's team could run it without me. Long-running work runs in the background so users never wait.

## Outcome
Within the first months the system shortened month-end close by 40 days. The client extended the engagement for a second phase.
