# Survey and NPS analysis for a manufacturing solo founder

**Client:** a solo founder in manufacturing · **Duration:** 3 weeks · **Year:** 2023

## The problem
The client's team had a recurring pain: open-text survey answers were never read. They needed something reliable that their non-technical staff could trust.

## What I built
I designed and delivered themed NPS drivers with a Tableau story. I worked with survey analysis, Python, data visualization and Tableau, and set it up so the client's team could run it without me. Long-running work runs in the background so users never wait.

## Outcome
Within the first months the system identified 31 top churn drivers acted on by product. The client extended the engagement for a second phase.
