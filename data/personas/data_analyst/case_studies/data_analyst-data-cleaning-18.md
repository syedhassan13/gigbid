# CRM data cleaning and deduplication for a e-commerce university lab

**Client:** a university lab in e-commerce · **Duration:** 4 weeks · **Year:** 2025

## The problem
The client's team had a recurring pain: duplicate contacts broke every campaign report. They needed something reliable that their non-technical staff could trust.

## What I built
I designed and delivered fuzzy-matched deduplication with a merge review sheet. I worked with data cleaning, Python, Excel and CRM, and set it up so the client's team could run it without me. Long-running work runs in the background so users never wait.

## Outcome
Within the first months the system removed 22% duplicate records. The client extended the engagement for a second phase.
