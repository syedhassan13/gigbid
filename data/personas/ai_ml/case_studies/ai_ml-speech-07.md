# Speech transcription and summarisation for a manufacturing mid-size agency

**Client:** a mid-size agency in manufacturing · **Duration:** 10 weeks · **Year:** 2025

## The problem
The client's team had a recurring pain: call recordings were never reviewed. They needed something reliable that their non-technical staff could trust.

## What I built
I designed and delivered automatic transcripts with summaries and action items. I worked with speech recognition, LLM, Python and AWS, and set it up so the client's team could run it without me. Long-running work runs in the background so users never wait.

## Outcome
Within the first months the system saved 35 analyst-hours per week. The client extended the engagement for a second phase.
