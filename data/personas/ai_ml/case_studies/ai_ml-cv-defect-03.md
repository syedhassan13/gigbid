# Visual defect detection for a fintech Series B scale-up

**Client:** a Series B scale-up in fintech · **Duration:** 8 weeks · **Year:** 2021

## The problem
The client's team had a recurring pain: manual inspection missed hairline defects on the line. They needed something reliable that their non-technical staff could trust.

## What I built
I designed and delivered a camera-based defect detector running on an edge box. I worked with computer vision, deep learning, PyTorch and Python, and set it up so the client's team could run it without me. Long-running work runs in the background so users never wait.

## Outcome
Within the first months the system caught 60% more defects than manual inspection. The client extended the engagement for a second phase.
