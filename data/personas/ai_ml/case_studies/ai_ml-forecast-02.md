# Demand forecasting model for a logistics seed-stage startup

**Client:** a seed-stage startup in logistics · **Duration:** 8 weeks · **Year:** 2021

## The problem
The client's team had a recurring pain: stock-outs and over-ordering every season. They needed something reliable that their non-technical staff could trust.

## What I built
I designed and delivered weekly SKU-level forecasts with prediction intervals. I worked with Python, time series, forecasting and machine learning, and set it up so the client's team could run it without me. Long-running work runs in the background so users never wait.

## Outcome
Within the first months the system reduced forecast error (MAPE) by 45%. The client extended the engagement for a second phase.
