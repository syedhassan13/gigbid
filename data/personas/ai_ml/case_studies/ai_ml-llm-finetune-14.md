# Fine-tuned LLM for domain extraction for a insurance consultancy

**Client:** a consultancy in insurance · **Duration:** 3 weeks · **Year:** 2023

## The problem
The client's team had a recurring pain: a general model kept missing domain-specific fields. They needed something reliable that their non-technical staff could trust.

## What I built
I designed and delivered a LoRA fine-tuned open model for structured extraction. I worked with LLM, fine-tuning, Hugging Face and Python, and set it up so the client's team could run it without me. Long-running work runs in the background so users never wait.

## Outcome
Within the first months the system raised field-level F1 by 22 points. The client extended the engagement for a second phase.
