# Churn prediction model in production for a manufacturing family-owned business

**Client:** a family-owned business in manufacturing · **Duration:** 4 weeks · **Year:** 2023

## The problem
The client's team had a recurring pain: the retention team only learned about churn after it happened. They needed something reliable that their non-technical staff could trust.

## What I built
I designed and delivered daily churn scores pushed to the CRM through a background job queue. I worked with machine learning, Python, MLOps and Docker, and set it up so the client's team could run it without me. Long-running work runs in the background so users never wait.

## Outcome
Within the first months the system helped retain 45% more at-risk accounts. The client extended the engagement for a second phase.
