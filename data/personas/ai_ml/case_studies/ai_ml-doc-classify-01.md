# Document classification pipeline for a manufacturing mid-size agency

**Client:** a mid-size agency in manufacturing · **Duration:** 10 weeks · **Year:** 2023

## The problem
The client's team had a recurring pain: thousands of incoming documents were routed by hand. They needed something reliable that their non-technical staff could trust.

## What I built
I designed and delivered an automatic classifier with a human review queue for low-confidence items. I worked with Python, NLP, scikit-learn and text classification, and set it up so the client's team could run it without me. Long-running work runs in the background so users never wait.

## Outcome
Within the first months the system automated 18% of routing at 96% precision. The client extended the engagement for a second phase.
