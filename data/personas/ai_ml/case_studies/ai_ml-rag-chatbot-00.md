# Retrieval-augmented support assistant for a education family-owned business

**Client:** a family-owned business in education · **Duration:** 3 weeks · **Year:** 2024

## The problem
The client's team had a recurring pain: support agents spent hours searching policy PDFs. They needed something reliable that their non-technical staff could trust.

## What I built
I designed and delivered a question-answering assistant over their knowledge base with cited answers. I worked with Python, LLM, RAG, FastAPI and embeddings, and set it up so the client's team could run it without me. Long-running work runs in the background so users never wait.

## Outcome
Within the first months the system cut average handling time by 18%. The client extended the engagement for a second phase.
