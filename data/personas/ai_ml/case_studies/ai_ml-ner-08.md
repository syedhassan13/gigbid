# Named-entity extraction from contracts for a nonprofit family-owned business

**Client:** a family-owned business in nonprofit · **Duration:** 6 weeks · **Year:** 2021

## The problem
The client's team had a recurring pain: paralegals copied clauses into spreadsheets. They needed something reliable that their non-technical staff could trust.

## What I built
I designed and delivered an extractor that pulls parties, dates and obligations into a structured table. I worked with NLP, named entity recognition, Python and LLM, and set it up so the client's team could run it without me. Long-running work runs in the background so users never wait.

## Outcome
Within the first months the system cut review time per contract by 31%. The client extended the engagement for a second phase.
