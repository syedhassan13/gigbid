# GigSim Marketplace Policy Pack (v1)

> Synthetic policy summary written for GigBid. Modelled on public marketplace
> terms; it is **not** Upwork's legal text. Machine-readable rules live in
> `rules.yaml` next to this file — the Compliance Guard loads them from there.

## P1. No off-platform contact or payment
Proposals and messages must not contain email addresses, phone numbers,
messaging handles (WhatsApp, Telegram, Skype, Discord), external payment
methods (PayPal, Wise, crypto, bank transfer), or invitations to continue
"outside the platform" before a contract exists.

## P2. No fabricated experience
Every claim of past work, skill, client, metric or credential must be
supported by the freelancer's portfolio. Unsupported claims must be removed.

## P3. No guarantees
Do not guarantee outcomes ("guaranteed results", "100% accuracy",
"first page of Google", "money-back"). Describe approach and evidence instead.

## P4. Fair use of automation
A human must approve every proposal and message. Automated tools may draft,
never send. Do not spam: at most the configured number of proposals per day.

## P5. No free work
Do not agree to unpaid "test tasks" that deliver production value. Flag jobs
that ask for free samples of real work.

## P6. Respectful, specific communication
No copy-paste generic openings, no pressure tactics, no discriminatory content.
